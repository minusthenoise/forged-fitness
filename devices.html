Samsung Health: import your data

file import

Samsung Health has no web login we can link to, so this is not a live account connection. You export your data from the app and import it here. Files are read in your browser only.

  1. Open Samsung Health on your phone and tap the ☰ menu (or the ⋮ on the Home tab), then Settings.
  2. Tap Download personal data and confirm with your Samsung account.
  3. When it finishes, find the folder Samsung Health in My Files (Internal storage › Download › Samsung Health). Zip it or pick the CSVs.
  4. Upload the ZIP, or the files named com.samsung.shealth.step_daily_trend…csv and com.samsung.shealth.exercise…csv.
